Home / How we work

Six phases. Every input and output named.

Each phase lists what we ask from you, what we do with it and the documents you receive. Nothing moves to the next phase until its outputs are reviewed with you.

  1. Assess

    We look at your company as it really runs: numbers, sales, operations, systems and people.

  2. Design

    We turn the diagnosis into a strategy and a roadmap you can fund.

  3. Plan

    Every initiative gets owners, a schedule, a budget, risks and a way to measure it.

  4. Deliver

    The right teams execute: software, engineering, production, logistics.

  5. Measure

    Results are compared with the starting baseline, not with promises.

  6. Improve

    What works becomes routine. What does not is corrected or stopped.

1

Assess

We build a complete picture of the company as it really runs, so the strategy starts from facts rather than impressions.

Company and governance

You provide

Company profile, organisation chart, ownership and decision roles, products and markets served, main clients.

We do

Document review, management interviews, governance and business-model mapping, positioning review.

You receive

Corporate assessment, business model canvas, governance map, SWOT.

Finance

You provide

Three to five years of accounts, revenue by client and product, fixed and variable costs, job margins, price lists, receivables and payables.

We do

Revenue, margin, EBITDA, cash-flow and working-capital analysis; pricing and profitability by client, product and job.

You receive

Financial diagnostic, margin analysis, cash-flow review, pricing review, financial baseline.

Sales

You provide

Client list, sales history, quotes won and lost, CRM export, pipeline, agents and distributors.

We do

Pipeline and funnel analysis, CRM data quality, conversion, retention and cross-selling potential.

You receive

Commercial assessment, pipeline report, customer ABC analysis, opportunity matrix.

Operations

You provide

Layout, routings, bills of materials, cycle and set-up times, quality and scrap data, suppliers, lead times, stock and shipping costs.

We do

Process mapping, capacity and OEE baseline, bottleneck, inventory, supplier and logistics analysis.

You receive

Process flow map, capacity model, bottleneck map, supplier risk matrix.

Technology

You provide

Software list, ERP/CRM/MES/PLM exports, critical spreadsheets, server and cloud set-up, backup and access policies.

We do

System map, digital maturity score, cybersecurity review, automation and AI use cases, integration gaps.

You receive

IT system map, digital maturity matrix, cybersecurity risk report, automation backlog.

People

You provide

Organisation chart, job descriptions, roles and KPIs, training plans, turnover, interviews with managers.

We do

Skills matrix, role and responsibility mapping, leadership and training-needs review.

You receive

Skills matrix, RACI draft, organisation gap report, training needs.

2

Design

The diagnosis becomes a strategy: where to grow, what to digitalise, what to fix in operations, how to sell and what it will cost.

Growth

You provide

Diagnostic report, current markets, target clients, capacity, management goals, growth budget.

We do

Market and segment analysis, product opportunities, partnerships, international scenarios.

You receive

Growth strategy plan, market opportunity matrix, expansion scenarios.

Digital

You provide

Technology assessment, current systems, manual workflows, IT priorities and budget, available data.

We do

Target architecture, data strategy, automation and AI roadmap, integration and cybersecurity plan.

You receive

Digital roadmap, target IT architecture, AI roadmap, automation backlog.

Operations

You provide

Operations assessment, quality problems, lead times, operating costs, investment plans.

We do

Lean roadmap, capacity planning, warehouse and supply-chain redesign, quality improvement.

You receive

Operational improvement plan, capacity plan, supplier strategy.

Commercial

You provide

Commercial assessment, CRM, pipeline, price lists and margins, lost quotes.

We do

Go-to-market and sales-process design, pricing, key-account and retention plans.

You receive

Commercial strategy, sales playbook, pricing framework, key-account plan.

Organisation

You provide

People assessment, current structure, staff budget, HR constraints.

We do

Target organisation, governance model, delegation matrix, KPIs by role.

You receive

Target operating model, organisation chart, RACI, delegation matrix.

Investment

You provide

Available budget, cash flow, supplier quotes, acceptable risk.

We do

CAPEX and OPEX analysis, ROI and payback modelling, business cases, prioritisation.

You receive

Investment plan, ROI model, business-case portfolio.

3

Plan

The approved strategy is broken down into work packages with owners, milestones, budget, risks and acceptance criteria.

Project governance

You provide

Approved roadmap, internal sponsor, stakeholders, approved budget, time constraints.

We do

Project charter, work breakdown structure, RACI, communication plan, reporting model.

You receive

Project charter, WBS, RACI matrix, governance calendar.

Resources

You provide

Staff availability, internal skills, calendar, existing partners.

We do

Skill matching, capacity allocation, make-or-buy, allocation of Next Mind units.

You receive

Resource plan, team structure, work-package map.

Risks

You provide

Known risks, legal and IT constraints, supplier dependencies, past problems.

We do

Risk identification and scoring, mitigation, contingency and escalation procedure.

You receive

Risk register, risk matrix, mitigation and contingency plans.

Procurement

You provide

Current suppliers and contracts, technical specifications, purchasing budget and policy.

We do

RFQ and RFP preparation, supplier shortlist and comparison, service levels.

You receive

Procurement plan, supplier scorecard, RFQ/RFP package.

Technology

You provide

System access, user requirements, data to migrate, required integrations.

We do

Solution architecture, functional and technical specifications, migration, test and go-live plans.

You receive

Solution architecture, test plan, go-live checklist.

Change

You provide

Affected users and roles, known resistance, training plans.

We do

Change impact analysis, stakeholder engagement, training and adoption measures.

You receive

Change management plan, training calendar, adoption plan.

4

Deliver

The responsible units execute while one coordinator keeps the client, the plan and the units aligned.

Coordination

You provide

Approved plan, available sponsor, timely decisions, deliverable approvals.

We do

Operational PMO, steering meetings, status reporting, issue and decision tracking.

You receive

Weekly status report, issue log, decision log, steering pack.

Software

You provide

Functional requirements, key users, data, system access, acceptance criteria.

We do

Analysis, design, development, configuration, integration, testing, training, release.

You receive

Software release, test report, user manual, acceptance report.

Engineering

You provide

Specifications, existing drawings, samples, materials, quality standards.

We do

CAD design, reverse engineering, bill of materials, design review, prototype support.

You receive

3D models, 2D drawings, BOM, technical package.

Manufacturing

You provide

Approved drawings, BOM, quantities, tolerances, quality and packaging requirements.

We do

Production planning, machining, welding, assembly, inspection, testing, packaging.

You receive

Inspection and test reports, packing list, completion report.

Trade and logistics

You provide

Delivery address, Incoterms, product codes, goods value, customs requirements, insurance.

We do

Supplier coordination, purchase orders, shipment booking, customs documents, tracking.

You receive

Shipping and customs documents, tracking report, delivery confirmation.

AI and automation

You provide

Manual processes, recurring documents, templates, datasets, validation rules.

We do

Use-case implementation, workflow automation, document processing, testing with users.

You receive

Automation workflow, AI test report, user guide.

5

Measure

Results are compared with the baseline set in the assessment, so improvement is measured rather than claimed.

Financial

You provide

Updated revenue, margins, costs, cash flow, budget against actual.

We do

ROI tracking, variance and cost analysis.

You receive

Financial dashboard, ROI report.

Commercial

You provide

Updated CRM, pipeline, quotes, orders, lost clients.

We do

Pipeline, conversion and retention tracking.

You receive

Sales dashboard, pipeline review.

Operational

You provide

Production, OEE, cycle and set-up times, scrap, deliveries.

We do

OEE, scrap, productivity and delivery review.

You receive

Operations dashboard, OEE report.

Project

You provide

Activity updates, approvals, open issues, change requests.

We do

Milestone, budget, risk and change control.

You receive

PMO dashboard, milestone report.

Digital and AI

You provide

User logs, usage data, errors, user feedback, automation logs.

We do

Adoption, data-quality, accuracy and time-saving analysis.

You receive

Adoption report, AI performance report.

6

Improve

The project becomes a routine: problems found, owners assigned, results reviewed every quarter and every year.

Continuous improvement

You provide

Updated KPIs, scrap and non-conformity data, operator suggestions.

We do

Root-cause analysis, kaizen workshops, standardisation, follow-up.

You receive

Improvement plan, kaizen board, updated procedures.

Knowledge

You provide

Procedures, manuals, technical documents, lessons learned.

We do

Knowledge-base design, procedure standardisation, document control.

You receive

Knowledge base, procedure library, lessons-learned record.

Reviews

You provide

Quarterly and annual results, open problems, new priorities.

We do

Quarterly business review and annual strategic review.

You receive

Review report, updated roadmap and targets.

You do not have to start at phase one.

A company with a clear technical need can start at Plan or Deliver, for example with an engineering package or a pilot workflow. We agree the entry point in the first conversation.
