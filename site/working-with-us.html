Home / Working with us

Start small. Expand only when it helps you.

Clients do not have to commit to a large programme before seeing what we deliver. Every engagement has a defined scope, an owner and a way to accept the result.

How a relationship usually grows

Each step happens only if the previous one delivered measurable value.

  1. Focused first engagement

    An assessment, a feasibility study, a CAD package, a software pilot, a sourcing analysis or one shipment.

  2. Validated project

    A larger piece of work built on what the first engagement showed.

  3. Integrated solution

    Several services combined under one plan where the outcome needs them.

  4. Recurring service

    Retained support, subscriptions, framework supply or logistics management.

  5. Long-term account

    Regular reviews, a stable team and a shared roadmap.

Engagement models

Prices depend on scope, risk and delivery obligations and are agreed per engagement.

Assessment

A diagnosis, report and roadmap for a fixed fee. Useful on its own, even if you do not continue with us.

Project or phase

Defined outputs, paid by phase or milestone. Changes go through an agreed change process.

Fixed support

Reserved capacity for a period, for example a project manager two days a week on site plus a weekly remote review.

Recurring package

Repeated outputs at an agreed frequency, such as monthly reporting on three jobs or four technical reviews a month.

Integrated programme

Several workstreams under one charter, one programme lead and one acceptance.

Production and supply

Deposits, milestone payments and technical approval before production release; warranty limits agreed in the contract.

What we need from you to start
