Home / Trust

Your information, designs and decisions stay under control.

How we handle confidentiality, intellectual property, data, AI, quality and third parties. We only state what is in place; certifications will be listed when they are obtained.

Confidentiality

Confidentiality terms are agreed before you share technical documents. They also bind our employees, consultants, suppliers and subcontractors.

Your intellectual property

Your drawings, specifications, data and custom code are governed by your contract. They never become Next Mind property by default and are never used for another client.

Our intellectual property

General methods, reusable software modules and engineering libraries belong to the Group. Contracts state clearly what is yours and what we keep.

Data access

Access is based on role and need. Customer information is not shared between units or partners without a contractual basis and an operational reason.

AI with human review

AI output used in deliverables, engineering decisions, contracts or specifications is reviewed by a qualified person before release. AI does not make material decisions.

Quality gates

Defined checks before releasing drawings, buying materials, starting production, shipping goods, deploying software or closing a project.

Qualified third parties

Suppliers and partners are assessed for capability, quality, reliability, confidentiality and compliance before they join a project.

Trade compliance

Counterparties, products and destinations are screened. We do not accept shipments where classification, end use or customer identity is unclear.

Ethics

Zero tolerance for bribery and corruption. Commissions are documented and approved. Concerns can be raised without retaliation.

Ask for our terms before you share documents.

We send the confidentiality, IP and data terms that apply to your engagement before any technical exchange.
