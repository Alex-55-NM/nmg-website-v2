Home / Services / Integrated Programmes

Several workstreams, one plan, one acceptance.

When the outcome needs engineering, digital, production and supply together, one programme lead connects them through shared milestones, interfaces and acceptance criteria.

When this helps

  • Each team has a plan, but the plans do not connect.
  • One team’s delay surfaces weeks later in another team’s work.
  • Nobody clearly owns the final handover.
  • You want one counterpart responsible for the whole result.

What we do

You can buy one of these activities or several connected ones. The scope is agreed with you before work starts.

Frame the programme

Objectives, scope boundaries, decision rights and measurable acceptance criteria.

Connect capabilities

Lead and supporting units assigned around the required outputs, with interfaces mapped.

Control execution

Milestones, interfaces, changes, risks and evidence managed through one integrated plan.

Accept and hand over

Deliverables verified with you; documentation, ownership and open actions transferred.

What you receive

Documents you can review, keep and use after we hand over. Results such as time or cost savings are measured against your own starting point.

  • Programme charter
  • Responsibility and interface map
  • Integrated delivery plan
  • Common risk and change log
  • Steering reports
  • Acceptance and handover pack

Who delivers

One company is your contract and your contact. The others work behind it on defined work packages.
