Home / Services / Digital Operations

One controlled workflow instead of spreadsheets and email approvals.

Process assessment, workflow design, integration, automation and AI, built with the people who do the work and measured after go-live.

When this helps

  • The same data is typed into several systems or spreadsheets.
  • Approvals get lost in email and nobody knows who must act next.
  • Reports arrive too late to change anything.
  • You are considering AI but have no data model or owner for the process.

What we do

You can buy one of these activities or several connected ones. The scope is agreed with you before work starts.

  1. 01

    Process assessment

    The actual process, data owners and recurring bottlenecks, mapped from real records.

  2. 02

    Workflow design

    Roles, data fields, approval rules and the decisions the workflow must support.

  3. 03

    Integration and automation

    Agreed systems connected and repeatable steps automated, tested with representative records and exceptions.

  4. 04

    Adoption and control

    User testing, training for process owners, monitoring of exceptions and adoption.

What you receive

Documents you can review, keep and use after we hand over. Results such as time or cost savings are measured against your own starting point.

  • Process and data map
  • Workflow specification
  • Tested integrations
  • Dashboard for the process owner
  • User guide and controls
  • Adoption measures against your baseline

Who delivers
