Home / Services / Engineering & Industrialisation

From a physical part or a concept to a design you can manufacture.

Reverse engineering, CAD, drawings, bills of materials, design for manufacture and assembly, prototypes and validation, released as one controlled package.

When this helps

  • A part must be reproduced, but the only reference is a worn sample.
  • Drawings are incomplete, or revisions are hard to trace.
  • Manufacturing problems are discovered at the supplier, too late.
  • A concept needs to become a prototype and then a producible product.

What we do

You can buy one of these activities or several connected ones. The scope is agreed with you before work starts.

Requirements and capture

Function, interfaces, materials and acceptance requirements clarified; geometry captured where useful.

Design and engineering

3D models, 2D drawings, tolerances and bills of materials under revision control.

Industrialisation

Design for manufacture and assembly, process choices and supplier feasibility.

Validation and release

Checks planned, results recorded and issues resolved before technical release.

What you receive

Documents you can review, keep and use after we hand over. Results such as time or cost savings are measured against your own starting point.

  • Requirements baseline
  • Controlled CAD models and drawings
  • Bill of materials
  • Industrialisation plan
  • Prototype or verification evidence
  • Engineering Release Pack

Who delivers

One company is your contract and your contact. The others work behind it on defined work packages.
