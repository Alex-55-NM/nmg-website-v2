Home / Services / Trade & Logistics

Goods, documents and suppliers on one visible route.

Supply planning, supplier follow-up, export documents and freight coordination with competent operators, from origin to the agreed delivery terms.

When this helps

  • Several suppliers must feed one delivery and their dates do not agree.
  • Shipment documents are incomplete or arrive late.
  • Responsibility for goods changes hands without anyone noticing.
  • Import, Incoterms and insurance questions are answered case by case.

What we do

You can buy one of these activities or several connected ones. The scope is agreed with you before work starts.

  1. 01

    Supply planning

    Quantities, lead times and delivery milestones aligned across suppliers.

  2. 02

    Supplier follow-up

    Commitments tracked and gaps escalated with the responsible supplier.

  3. 03

    Shipment readiness

    Packing, documentation, freight booking and customs interfaces coordinated with competent operators.

  4. 04

    Delivery and closure

    Milestones followed, receipt confirmed and quantity or document discrepancies closed.

What you receive

Documents you can review, keep and use after we hand over. Results such as time or cost savings are measured against your own starting point.

  • Supply schedule
  • Supplier action log
  • Shipment document checklist
  • Tracking report
  • Delivery confirmation
  • Closure record
