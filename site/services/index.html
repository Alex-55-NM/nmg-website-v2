Six services. Buy one, or connect them.

Each service has its own first deliverable. When a result needs more than one discipline, Integrated Programmes put them under one plan and one acceptance.

ServiceWhat it coversUsual first engagement

Strategy & Growth

Market analysis, go-to-market, portfolio and new-country entry for industrial companies, with a roadmap someone is responsible for carrying out.

Market and growth assessmenttwo to three candidate markets or segments compared, with a recommendation to enter, wait or drop.

Digital Operations

Process assessment, workflow design, integration, automation and AI, built with the people who do the work and measured after go-live.

Programme charterscope, sponsor, decision rights, units involved and the integrated plan, approved before execution starts.

Three ways the offer is organised

The level depends on your need, budget and how far you want to go. We do not push clients toward the largest one.

A focused service

One defined piece of work: an assessment, a CAD package, a software module, a prototype, a production order or one shipment.

An integrated solution

Two or more services combined to solve a broader problem, such as engineering and production, or consulting and software.

A strategic account

Long-term support across several workstreams: retained consulting, subscriptions, recurring production and logistics, with regular performance reviews.

Strategy, digital, engineering, production and trade capabilities, coordinated as one route from your need to an accepted result.
