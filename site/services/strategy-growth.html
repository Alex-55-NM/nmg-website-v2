Home / Services / Strategy & Growth

Decide where to grow before you commit the organisation.

Market analysis, go-to-market, portfolio and new-country entry for industrial companies, with a roadmap someone is responsible for carrying out.

When this helps

  • You want to export or enter a new country, but have no validated target segment or channel.
  • Sales depend on a few historic relationships and a few people.
  • You have several growth ideas and not enough people or money to pursue all of them.
  • Growth plans have been written before, but nobody turned them into work packages.

What we do

You can buy one of these activities or several connected ones. The scope is agreed with you before work starts.

  1. 01

    Market and segment analysis

    Demand signals, competitors, customer requirements and the cost of serving each market, with assumptions kept separate from verified evidence.

  2. 02

    Go-to-market

    Target accounts, channels, positioning, sales process and the people who will run them.

  3. 03

    Portfolio and business model

    The offer, its value to the client, the revenue logic and what delivery will require.

  4. 04

    International expansion

    Entry routes, local partners and operating constraints, screened for payment, regulatory and logistics risk.

What you receive

Documents you can review, keep and use after we hand over. Results such as time or cost savings are measured against your own starting point.

  • Prioritised opportunity list
  • Comparison of entry routes
  • Commercial plan with owners
  • Execution roadmap
  • Success measures agreed with you
  • Handover pack with open actions

Who delivers

One company is your contract and your contact. The others work behind it on defined work packages.

Solutions · Italy

Leads for European clients and coordinates the work.

Consulting · UAE

Leads outside Europe and contributes analysis and international development.

How it usually starts

Market and growth assessment: two to three candidate markets or segments compared, with a recommendation to enter, wait or drop.
