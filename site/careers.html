How we hire

The same standards in every country.

  1. A written role

    Every opening states the outcomes expected in the first year and the skills that will be assessed, separating essential from trainable.

  2. A practical exercise

    A work sample, case or trade test close to the real job, assessed by a specialist.

  3. A structured panel

    The same core questions for every candidate. At least two trained assessors score independently before they discuss.

  4. Clear communication

    Application confirmed within one business day and a status update at least every five business days while your process is active.
