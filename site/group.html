One group, six companies, one way of working.

Each company has one economic job. Together they cover the route from a client’s need to delivery, without duplicating the same capability in several countries.

How the Group is organised

Three layers: ownership and governance, the companies you deal with, and the delivery centres behind them.

Ownership and governance

Cyprus

Next Mind Holding

Owns the operating companies, the brand, methods and shared technology. Allocates capital and sets common rules for finance, quality, data and compliance.

Your interface

Italy

Next Mind Solutions

European client relationships, solution design, proposals, project coordination and acceptance. Can act as your European contracting entity.

United Arab Emirates

Next Mind Consulting

Clients outside Europe: strategy, market entry, transformation programmes, PMO and international business development.

Delivery centres

India

Next Mind Platform

Software architecture, the Group platform, AI and automation, integration, cybersecurity and support.

India

Next Mind Engineering & Production

Two divisions with separate budgets and quality records: engineering and product development; manufacturing and industrial operations.

India

Next Mind Trade & Logistics

Shipping planning, export documents, Incoterms, freight-forwarder coordination, tracking and supplier follow-up.

Planned

United States

North America hub

A market-facing company for North American clients, to be set up only when demand, revenue and local needs justify it. Until then, North American clients are served by Consulting or Solutions.

The Group is being built in stages. A company is created only when there is a measurable business reason for it, not to create the appearance of scale.

Who you deal with

Every client has one commercial owner. The units behind it work on defined work packages and never contact you outside the agreed process.

Why the work is split this way

Close to the client

Italy and the UAE keep relationships, language, contracts and expectations local. You have one counterpart from the first meeting to acceptance.

Capability in one place

Software, engineering, manufacturing and trade execution are concentrated in India instead of being rebuilt in each country, which keeps quality and methods consistent.

Controlled, not offshored

The value is not only lower cost. It is technical capability combined with commercial coordination, quality control, visibility and one accountable project lead.

Strategy, digital, engineering, production and trade capabilities, coordinated as one route from your need to an accepted result.
