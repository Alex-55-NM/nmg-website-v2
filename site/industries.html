Home / Industries

Industrial companies with technical, operational or cross-border complexity.

Industry matters, but the stronger signal is the type of problem: not enough coordination capacity, technical complexity, delivery across borders or several workstreams at once.

Sectors we focus on

We expand into new sectors only once we can show relevant references, technical competence and quality standards.

Industrial machinery and equipment

Special-purpose machinery, production equipment, automation systems and assemblies.

Typically needed: CAD and engineering packages, industrialisation, component production, supplier coordination, delivery abroad.

Material handling and intralogistics

Handling equipment, intralogistics systems and industrial mobility.

Typically needed: Engineering, industrial design, manufacturing, automation and software, spare parts and after-sales coordination.

Packaging, food-processing and line equipment

Packaging machinery, food-processing equipment and production-line systems.

Typically needed: Mechanical engineering, production support, spare parts, quality records, export documents.

Industrial automation and connected operations

Companies digitalising engineering, production and supplier information.

Typically needed: Workflow automation, engineering-document control, production visibility, reporting, AI use cases.

Precision components and mobility

Tooling, fixtures, technical components and assemblies. Highly regulated supply chains only once quality systems and references are in place.

Typically needed: Engineering services, prototypes, controlled production support.

Supply chain and trade

Companies with many suppliers, cross-border flows and unreliable export processes.

Typically needed: Supplier coordination, sourcing support, export documents, shipment visibility.

Renewable-energy equipment

Selected equipment and infrastructure components, where technical fit and compliance are clear.

Typically needed: Engineering, industrialisation, component supply, trade coordination.

Who we serve first

Industrial SMEs and mid-sized companies are the core: big enough to need more than one specialist, not big enough to build every capability in-house.

A good fit

  • Industrial SMEs and mid-market manufacturers
  • OEMs, machinery builders and component producers
  • Companies entering new markets or restructuring international operations
  • Technology-enabled industrial companies
  • Companies with fragmented supply, engineering or trade processes

Usually not a fit

  • Purely price-driven procurement with no interest in coordination
  • Requests for broad capability without agreed scope or commercial terms
  • Low-value transactional orders

Where we start

We lead with the service that creates the lowest barrier and the clearest value, then expand only if it improves the outcome.
